# LUNA — Detector Calibration and Nuclear-Reaction Analysis

**INFN · Turin and Gran Sasso, Italy · Summer 2022**

LUNA measures nuclear reactions that occur inside stars. Its underground accelerator reduces background signals, allowing very weak reactions to be studied.

## My Contribution

- Developed Python/CERN ROOT tools to calibrate detector spectra and separate overlapping gamma-ray peaks with multi-Gaussian fits.
- Checked accelerator power logs against hourly spectra to exclude invalid intervals and recover usable data from interrupted runs.
- Combined calibrated spectra and analyzed the direct-capture signal from neon-20; participated in beam-energy scans at Gran Sasso.
